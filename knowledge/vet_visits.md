# Veterinary Checkups and Vet Visits

Adult dogs and cats generally benefit from an annual wellness exam, even when
they appear healthy, since many conditions (dental disease, early kidney
issues, weight changes) are easier to catch and treat before symptoms become
obvious. Senior pets (dogs 7+, cats 11+) are usually better served by twice-
yearly checkups, since age-related conditions can progress faster between
visits.

Bring a written or mental list of any behavior changes since the last visit —
appetite shifts, altered water intake, new limping, changes in litter box or
bathroom habits, or new lumps — since owners often notice subtle changes vets
wouldn't catch in a single exam. Vaccination schedules vary by pet, location,
and lifestyle (indoor vs. outdoor cats, for example), so following your vet's
specific recommended schedule matters more than a generic one-size-fits-all
timeline.

Pets can experience stress or anxiety around vet visits. Arriving a few
minutes early, bringing a favorite treat, and keeping cats in a covered
carrier can reduce stress for both the pet and the visit's accuracy, since
elevated stress can temporarily affect readings like heart rate and blood
pressure.
